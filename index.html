For corporate importers, B2B procurement managers, and e-commerce enterprises shipping commercial cargo from the United States to the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA), navigating local customs regulations is essential to avoid impounded shipments and demurrage penalties.

What is DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)?

Under DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) Incoterms, AQX Logistics assumes complete legal and operational responsibility for the cargo from our Los Angeles (LAX) export gateway until it is delivered to your business doorstep in Riyadh, Jeddah, or Dammam. All export documentation, international air transit, 15% ZATCA VAT, and customs duties are fully pre-cleared.

Key Regulatory Authorities in Saudi Arabia

  • ZATCA (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority): Enforces the 15% standard Value Added Tax (VAT) and customs tariffs based on HS codes. Requires compliant e-invoicing.
  • SFDA (Saudi Food and Drug Authority): Mandates product registration and laboratory certification for cosmetics, skincare, vitamins, and medical devices.
  • SABER Platform: Online conformity verification system for regulated industrial, electronic, and consumer goods.
